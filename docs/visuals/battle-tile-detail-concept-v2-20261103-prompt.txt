Use case: ui-mockup.
Create ONE new, art-directed redesign reference for the DiceLand battle-tile details panel. A finished ultrawide mobile game screen, about 2.17:1. The user rejected the previous concept because it lacked visual design, hierarchy and a clear focal point. This new version must feel distinctly redesigned. Remove the entire action-point cost section.

INPUT ROLES:
Image 1: current game context and existing functional information. Use its forest map, left HUD, traveler and map tiles as the setting.
Image 2: approved visual language: dark forest-green materials, warm ivory Chinese type, aged brass workmanship. Use its palette and craftsmanship without copying its rectangular reading-panel layout.

CREATIVE DIRECTION:
Design an elegant heraldic encounter placard floating on the RIGHT side of the map. Strong silhouette, one memorable central composition, generous intentional negative space and controlled detail. The panel is about 30% of screen width and 76% of screen height, with air around it. Deep blackened pine-green leather, delicate antique brass edge, gently clipped shoulders and a small pointed lower crest. A narrow worn oxblood velvet ribbon descends behind its upper-left shoulder. Restrained relief details at the top and bottom, not repeated floral flourishes. Its internal background has a nearly invisible large engraved crossed-swords watermark.
The new focal point is a bespoke heraldic crossed-swords emblem integrated with the title. An aged brass shield medallion with crossed steel blades and subtle red enamel sits slightly off-center, partly breaking the upper frame. It should be handsome painted game UI ornament, not a creature illustration. Elegant overlapping depth between emblem, ribbon and frame.
Keep the overall presentation practical and reusable as an actual game UI.

TYPOGRAPHY AND VISUAL HIERARCHY:
Give the title and danger assessment a deliberate clear relationship.
- Large, beautifully spaced ivory “精英战斗” is the dominant text, directly beneath and slightly to the right of the crest. Refined, weighty Chinese serif display lettering with accurate readable characters. Do not use gigantic blunt block lettering.
- A small warm-brass eyebrow “外围 · [3,5]” sits above the title.
- Beneath the title, leave breathing room, then an asymmetrical danger composition: tiny muted “危险度” above a confident medium-large vermilion “吃力” at left, and a much quieter small “2/8” aligned to its baseline at right. The title must remain visually stronger than “吃力”. Use a slim interrupted brass rule or tiny subtle notches as ornament, not a row of large glowing diamonds or a dashboard meter.
- The attribute is a quiet short line set lower and inset a little farther right: small “属性倾向”, followed by a fine flame glyph and “炎” in restrained amber. This is clearly secondary to battle type and danger.
- Two small muted status words “未探索 · 未解决” sit on a single low-key line near the foot of the information area. They are not boxed pills.

ACTIONS:
One refined full-width primary button “进入战斗” near the bottom: dark oxblood enamel, thin brass edge, a small steel crossed-swords icon, warm ivory text, subtle highlight on the top bevel. Comfortable touch height without overwhelming the title.
Below it, the secondary action “返回地图” is an understated ivory text action with a small return arrow and a short fine underline, not a second large competing rectangular button.
The interior should feel composed around the heraldic identity, with asymmetric alignment and varied spacing. Do not partition every item into a separate full-width bordered row. No stacked dashboard cards. Avoid overfilling the surface with many rules and borders.
